Lemon Launcher is the smallest, plainest Android TV home screen that could work: a black screen, a text list of the apps and inputs you pick, and nothing else.

Features:

* Under 17KB, no libraries, no AndroidX, no Kotlin runtime
* Zero background components — nothing runs unless you're looking at it
* Text-only, no icons to load or decode
* Works from Android 5.0 (API 21) up
* Long press to open the add/remove picker, listing every installed app, TV input (HDMI, etc.), and a couple of built-in actions (Settings, Free Up Memory)
* Short press an item to launch it, long press again to remove it from your list

No ads, no tracking, no network permission at all. Fully open source.
